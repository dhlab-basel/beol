Bernoulli Euler Digital

The Bernoulli-Euler Digital represents a notable expansion of the Bernoulli-Euler Online (BEOL) project. This platform serves as a dedicated research hub, focusing on exploring early modern mathematics and science. Its primary objective is to seamlessly integrate the extensive works and correspondences of the prominent members of the Bernoulli dynasty and the influential mathematician Leonhard Euler.
